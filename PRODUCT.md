# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The owner manages one personal profile. Visitors view the public profile and follow the owner's social or custom links.

## Product Purpose

Provide one public, personal bio page that brings the owner's introduction and online links together. The owner can update its content and appearance from a private dashboard.

## Positioning

A single-owner identity page with a private editor, rather than a multi-user link-in-bio platform.

## Operating Context

The owner signs in to update profile details, media, theme, and links. Visitors open the public page on desktop or mobile and follow the links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public profile is visitor-accessible; only the owner may edit it.
- Updates are made through an authenticated dashboard.
- The public profile follows the owner's supplied dark, centered profile reference while retaining original branding and assets.
- The owner may use a muted background video and may explicitly link a Discord identity for a public username/avatar link.
- Discord integration requests identity only; it does not display online status or use a bot.
- Profile media uploads are limited to 5 MB for images and 20 MB for MP4/WebM video.
- The project starts without an existing codebase, hosting account, domain, user content, or user-provided media.
- Prefer free hosting and a free subdomain; avoid paid services at this stage.
- Advanced features such as audio, online-presence tracking, analytics, template marketplaces, and public user registration are not part of the initial scope.
- guns.lol is a functional reference only; do not copy its branding, exact layout, text, or proprietary assets.
- Hosting and free-tier providers may impose limits or pause inactive projects.

## Evidence on Hand

No owner name, bio, links, photographs, or other personal profile content has been provided. Do not invent personal claims or credentials; provide clearly labeled editable placeholders.

## Product Principles

- Keep the page personal and focused on one identity.
- Make the public profile easy to understand and use on mobile.
- Make every content change owner-controlled and securely persisted.
- Prefer a useful, maintainable free setup over platform features the owner does not need.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard-operable interactions, visible focus states, and sufficient text contrast.
